# Globe illustration source

land-source.geojson is the Natural Earth 1:110m land dataset, retrieved from:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Natural Earth geographic data are public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

land-points.json samples points inside those land polygons at approximately
1.55 degrees latitude, with longitude spacing adjusted for latitude. Coordinates
are longitude, latitude. The points form the decorative Earth canvas.

The satellite orbit, surrounding ticks and stars are conceptual decoration.
They do not represent a mission trajectory, measurements, studies, or telemetry.
